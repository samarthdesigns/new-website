EXPERIENCES

I bring consumer-grade craft to software people are forced to use. Enterprise tools, internal dashboards, admin panels; the products people sit inside for eight hours a day and quietly resent. That's where I've spent most of my career, and it's the most interesting design problem I know: nobody chose this software, they just have to live in it.

SYSTEM BEHIND IT

The other half is design systems; tokens, components, documentation, the infrastructure that decides whether a product stays coherent as it grows. I've built five. One is used by 250+ designers across 12 teams. Another underpins demos that have supported over $100M in enterprise deals.
