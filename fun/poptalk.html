Overview

I spend half my day on Google Meet calls. The call lives in one tab, and my work lives in all the others. Meet's pop-out window was supposed to help, but it just parks a grid of faces on top of whatever I'm doing.

So I built PopTalk: a tiny island for call controls at the top of my screen, and a few bubbles in the corner that show who's talking.

Solution

A call that floats instead of sitting in a window

The island stays small and black until I hover over it, then opens into mute, camera, reactions and leave. The person talking gets a video bubble, and the bubbles swap places when someone new speaks. When I move my cursor near them, they slide out of the way.

Behind the build

Me designing, Claude coding

I designed each piece, and Claude wrote the code. I'd try a build on a real call, tell it what felt off, like a spring that was too bouncy or a bubble in the wrong spot, and go again. Then I made a launch video the same way.

Reflection

Small enough to forget it's there

That was the whole goal. I barely notice PopTalk until someone starts talking, and that's how I know it works.

Quick start

  1. Add the extension to Chrome, Brave or Edge.
  2. Optional: install the desktop app so the bubbles float over every app.
  3. Join a Google Meet call and switch tabs. PopTalk takes over. Alt+P opens it any time.
