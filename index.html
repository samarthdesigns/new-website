About Me

I'm Samarth; a designer with 5+ years of experience who comes from a background of development and marketing. Funnily enough the first career choice was to code but then I told what I do in code is focusing more on how users use it; so I pivoted and what a pivot it was (Chandler, this is how you PIVOT!).

Since then I have spent my life designing clean and simpler experiences with a big focus on visual balance with UX. With time I have explored the operations behind designing these experiences and provide an expertise in design systems from a traditional sense but also with AI.
