About Me

I'm Samarth, a designer with 7+ years of experience who comes from a background in development and marketing. Funnily enough, my first career choice was to code, but I realized that what I cared about most in code was how people use it. So I pivoted, and what a pivot it was (Chandler, this is how you PIVOT!).

Since then I have spent my career designing clean, simple experiences with a big focus on balancing visuals and UX. Over time I have explored the operations behind designing these experiences, and I bring expertise in design systems, both the traditional kind and AI-native ones.
